# Compliance, Originality & Disclaimers

## 1. Simulation Disclaimer
StockSense-AI is strictly a **simulated paper trading and research application**.
- No real monetary deposits or withdrawals are supported.
- All capital, margins, orders, profits, and losses displayed in the platform are purely virtual and hypothetical.
- StockSense-AI is **not** a SEBI-registered broker, investment advisor, or research analyst. No content or AI output constitutes financial advice.
